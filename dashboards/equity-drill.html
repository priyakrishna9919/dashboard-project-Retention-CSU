Equity Focus
−12 pts
The retention equity gap between Black/African American and White students at CSU is 12 percentage points (63% vs 75%). This gap is larger when students are also Pell-eligible and first-generation. Cross-tabulating these three dimensions reveals the highest-risk student segment: Black/AA, Pell-eligible, first-generation students retain at an estimated 51%. This dashboard makes that intersection visible and actionable.
