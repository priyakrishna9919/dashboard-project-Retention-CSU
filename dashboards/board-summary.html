Board narrative — Fall 2024
📈
Enrollment — stable with headwinds
Total enrollment of 14,210 reflects a 1.8% decline versus Fall 2023, consistent with regional demographic trends. New student enrollment rose 2.1%, signaling improving recruitment performance. Graduate enrollment declined 5% — a priority area for targeted investment.
⚠ Monitor graduate pipeline
🎓
Retention & completion — improving
The 6-year graduation rate reached 47.2%, up 1.2 percentage points — the fourth consecutive year of improvement. First-year retention held steady at 72.4%, on par with Ohio urban peer average. Part-time student retention (est. 55%) remains the largest equity gap requiring intervention.
✓ Grad rate positive trajectory
🌍
Access & diversity — mission-aligned
CSU continues to serve its urban access mission: approximately 42% of students are Pell-eligible, 18.4% identify as Black/African American, and international enrollment grew 3.2% post-COVID. Cuyahoga County provides ~62% of Ohio resident students, reflecting CSU's deep regional roots.
